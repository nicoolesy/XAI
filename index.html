Source & Collect

Gather and license raw text at scale — web crawls, books, code, and scientific papers. Frontier corpora now reach trillions of tokens.

Clean & Tokenize

Deduplicate, filter for quality and safety, then split text into tokens — the subword units the model actually reads. Garbage in, garbage out.

Compute Core03 / 07

Pretrain

The main event: next-token prediction over the entire corpus on thousands of GPUs for weeks or months. Almost all the bill lives here.

GPT-4 ≈ 25,000 A100s · Llama 3 405B ≈ 16,000 H100s

Supervised Fine-Tune

Train the base model on curated instruction–response pairs so it stops merely predicting text and starts following directions.

Align & Tune05 / 07

Reward Modeling

Humans rank competing responses; a separate reward model learns to predict those human preferences. This is the most people-intensive step.

Human annotators, not GPUs, are the bottleneck

Align & Tune06 / 07

RLHF / Alignment

Optimize the model against the reward model (PPO, DPO) to make it more helpful, honest, and harmless without breaking its core abilities.

Evaluate & Deploy

Benchmark, red-team for safety, then serve to users. Over a model's lifetime, this inference stage can consume more energy than training ever did.

Inference can reach ~90% of a model's lifetime energy
